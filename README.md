# Budapest music font

The **Budapest music font** is a music notation font digitalized from the plate engraving font used in **Editio Musica Budapest** (EMB)'s complete edition of **Liszt**, featuring dense black noteheads and relatively thin strokes.

The font is licensed under [SIL Open Font License 1.1](LICENSE.md). Any individual or enterprise can use - including commercial use - this font for free without notifying the author otherwise.

---

**Note**: There exists computer-made scores in EMB's complete edition of Liszt, which uses the Pertrucci music font, one of the built-in notation fonts in Finale. Budapest music font is the digital replication of EMB's plate engraving font, which differs from Pertrucci.
